# Datasheet

Reference tables in the style of a commercial converter datasheet
(structure follows TI's ADS131A04). One honest difference: a vendor
datasheet's numbers come from characterizing thousands of parts over
temperature; **every number here is a simulation output of this
repository's CI pipeline** — schematic-level (tier 1/2) or extracted
from the layout (PEX), all at 27 °C, typical process corner unless
stated. Silicon comes back from the TTSKY26c shuttle later.

## Features

- 1st-order continuous-time ΣΔ modulator, 1-bit, 50 MHz clock
- 0–3.3 V single-ended input range, ratiometric to the analog supply
- Complementary raw bitstream outputs (decimate off-chip: ~1 MHz
  bandwidth at OSR 25, ~100 kHz at OSR 250)
- Return-to-zero resistive feedback DAC — immune to pattern-dependent
  edge errors by construction
- On-chip references, bias, clock level shifter; single external clock
- TinyTapeout 2×2 analog tile, sky130A

## Pin functions

| Pin | Name | I/O | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ua[0]` | VIN | analog in | Signal input, 0–3.3 V about the 1.65 V mid-scale, through the on-chip 132 kΩ input resistor into the modulator's virtual ground. Out-of-range input saturates the bitstream (no damage 0–3.3 V); recovery is immediate. |
| `ua[1]` | INT | analog out | Integrator (OTA) output monitor for silicon debug. High-impedance node — observe with ≥1 MΩ ∕ ≤10 pF. Leave open in normal use; the ADC operates without it. |
| `clk` | CLK | digital in | Modulator clock, 50 MHz nominal, 1.8 V CMOS; level-shifted to 3.3 V on-chip. Supply a clean low-jitter clock — RZ feedback doubles jitter sensitivity. |
| `uo[0]` | Q | digital out | Modulator bitstream, retimed, 1.8 V CMOS. One symbol per clock period; capture synchronously and decimate off-chip. |
| `uo[1]` | Q̄ | digital out | Complement of Q. Together with `uo[0]` forms a pseudo-differential pair for a terminated receiver ("pseudo-LVDS"). |
| `VAPWR` | — | power | 3.3 V analog supply. References, input range and mid-scale are ratiometric to it. |
| `VDPWR` | — | power | 1.8 V digital supply (level-shifter input stage, output drivers). |
| `VGND` | — | ground | Common ground. |
| `ua[2..5]` | — | — | Unused analog pins (not connected). |
| `ui_in[7:0]`, `uio[7:0]`, `uo[7:2]`, `rst_n`, `ena` | — | — | Unused digital pins. The design has no reset or enable — it is alive whenever powered and clocked. |

## Typical application

Drive VIN from a source impedance well below 132 kΩ (or buffer
externally); the input is DC-coupled, so a source at a different
common mode needs level shifting or AC coupling with a bias to
1.65 V. Feed a clean 50 MHz clock; terminate Q/Q̄ into a differential
or single-ended receiver at the FPGA; capture one bit per clock on
the same clock domain and decimate digitally (sinc³ or better). The
reference decimation targets are OSR 25 for the ~1 MHz protection
path and OSR 250 for the ~100 kHz measurement path — both from the
same bitstream, concurrently.
